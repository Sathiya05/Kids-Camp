What age groups do you accept?

We welcome campers aged 5 to 15. Groups are split by age so activities, pace and supervision are always age-appropriate.

How safe is the camp?

Safety comes first. We have a certified staff-to-child ratio, secure premises, first-aid on hand, and mandatory parent check-in/out.

Are meals and snacks included?

Yes. Full-day and overnight plans include nutritious, kid-approved meals and daily snacks. We can accommodate most dietary needs on request.

What should my child bring?

Comfortable clothes, a water bottle, sunscreen, and a hat. We provide all activity materials, equipment and safety gear.
