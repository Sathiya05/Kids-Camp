Help Us Help You

Parent & Camper Guidelines

A few simple steps keep every child safe and comfortable at camp. Please review these before your first day.

  • Register health details

    Share allergies, medications and emergency contacts on signup.

  • Share the pickup plan

    Authorize relatives or nannies in advance for smooth, secure checkout.

  • Pack the essentials

    Water bottle, sun protection, hat and comfortable clothes each day.

  • Arrive on time

    Check-in with photo ID at the gate; call ahead if you'll be late.

Your Questions

Safety FAQ

What if my child has allergies?

We maintain an allergy-aware menu and an action plan for every child. Our staff is trained to recognize and respond to reactions quickly.

How does pickup verification work?

The pickup person must show photo ID that matches your authorized list. We never release a child without verified confirmation.

Is the site fully enclosed?

Yes. Activity and play areas are fully fenced with single supervised access points and 24/7 video monitoring.

What happens in bad weather?

We have covered indoor spaces and move activities indoors seamlessly. Severe weather plans are ready well in advance.
