Fees FAQ

Are there any hidden charges?

No. Every plan is all-inclusive. Activities, materials, meals (per plan), staff and insurance are all covered in the price.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes! Split your summer fee into 2-3 easy installments at no extra cost.

What's your refund policy?

Cancel up to 7 days before your session for a full refund. After that, we offer a credit toward any future camp.

Do discounts combine?

You can combine sibling and early-bird offers. Full-summer and referral credits stack too — we're happy to show you your best deal.
